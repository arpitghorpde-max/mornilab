1. Sign in as a Principal and confirm **Reports & analytics** appears in the sidebar.
2. Open Overview and confirm KPI cards load without API errors.
3. Change Academic Year, Class, Skill, Teacher, Stage and date filters one at a time; confirm reports refresh.
4. Check Students report: student name, class, XP, progress, projects, badges, trophies, certificates and last activity.
5. Check Classes report: students, teachers, class teacher, projects, average XP, progress and certificates.
6. Check Skills report: students enrolled, average progress, projects, XP and three-stage completion counts.
7. Check Teachers report: class assignments, teaching areas, reviewed projects, supported students and XP awarded.
8. Check Projects report and confirm teacher-verified completed work appears with evidence status and reviewer.
9. Check Achievements and Certificates reports.
10. Download a CSV and open it in Excel.
11. Use Print / Save PDF and verify the report prints cleanly.
12. Sign in as Founder, open Reports & analytics, confirm all-school comparison appears, then open one school report.
13. Confirm a Principal cannot access another school's reporting data.
14. Regression-check learning, XP, portfolio, academic structure, bulk management and billing.
